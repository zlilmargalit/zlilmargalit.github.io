Usage Information

University Study Agent is a private, owner-operated study tool. It is not offered to the public and does not accept third-party accounts. Its output is a convenience aid; the owner should verify assignments and deadlines against original course announcements. Access can be stopped by turning off the workflow or revoking the Google connection.

The tool does not send email, submit coursework, or delete files. See the privacy policy for details about data processing.
